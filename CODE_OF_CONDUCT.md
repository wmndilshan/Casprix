# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must communicate respectfully, focus criticism on technical work,
welcome people of different backgrounds and experience levels, and avoid
harassment, threats, discrimination, or publication of private information.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's maintainers or
GitHub moderation tools. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the
project or its community.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other spaces representing the Casprix project.
